## Vexara - Alpha Patch Notes v0.17

New onboarding tips for fresh players, an Espionage log in Combat Logs,
daily galaxy events firing more often, and a richer "While You Were Away"
summary.

---

### Beginner tips overlay

New players now get a short guided tour on their first few sessions , a
spotlight highlights the relevant part of the screen and a tooltip explains
what to do and why. There are five steps in total: creating your nexus,
upgrading a building, training troops, launching your first strike, and
joining a faction.

The overlay tracks your progress across sessions. Once a step is completed
it doesn't come back. You can dismiss any tip for the current session with
the **×** button, or turn them off permanently with **Disable tips** if you
already know what you're doing.

### Espionage log in Combat Logs

The Combat Logs page now has two tabs: **Combat** (unchanged) and
**Espionage**. The Espionage tab shows every completed spy mission you've
sent across all your nexuses , target name, coordinates, mission type, and
whether the operative succeeded or failed. Logs are permanent and paginate
in batches of 50.

### Daily galaxy events fire more often

The chance of a galaxy event firing each day has been raised from **65% to
75%**. Events (resource boosts, military surges, recruitment drives, etc.)
should feel less sporadic now.

### While You Were Away - more context

The return summary has been expanded with three new sections:

- **Faction wars** , if your faction declared or was declared upon while
  you were offline, it shows up here with both faction names and the
  current war status.
- **Nearby conquests** , any nexus conquered within 10 tiles of one of
  your nexuses during your absence is listed with its coordinates, so you
  can assess the threat on the map.
- **Your rank** , your current leaderboard position and total influence are
  shown at the bottom of the summary as a quick pulse check.

### Referral link click tracking

When someone follows your personal invite link and lands on the
registration page, that click is now counted and shown in your
**Referrals** tab alongside your referred players list.

---

A lot of this patch is quality-of-life , getting new players up to speed
faster, giving returning players more to wake up to, and making the
intelligence side of the game feel like a real log rather than a throwaway
screen.
