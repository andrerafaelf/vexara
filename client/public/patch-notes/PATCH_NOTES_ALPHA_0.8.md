## Vexara - Alpha Patch Notes v0.8

Daily quests and streaks. Faction cap at 10. New Deployed Forces tab.
Conquered nexus alert. Travel time shown before you send. Combat reports now appear the
moment the strike lands, not when troops walk home.

---

### Daily quests and streaks

Three quests rotate every day , train troops, upgrade a building, launch
an attack, send a scout, or just log in. Complete all three to collect
resource rewards deposited to your strongest nexus.

Consecutive days build a streak. The longer the streak, the bigger the
reward multiplier , up to 2x at seven days. Miss a day and it resets.
A warning banner appears on the overview tab when your streak is about
to expire so you don't lose it by accident.

Your current streak and personal best are shown on your profile page
with a subtle heat effect behind the counter.

---

### Conquered nexus alert

When an enemy takes one of your nexuses, a full-screen modal now fires
in real time , no more silently losing a nexus while you're tabbed into
the map or faction chat. Shows the nexus name and who took it.

---

### Deployed Forces tab

New tab on every nexus. Shows two things in one place:

- **Troops in combat** , every outgoing attack and scout with destination,
  troop breakdown, mission type, and a live countdown.
- **Troop transfers** , traveling, stationed, and returning transfers with
  recall and send-back actions.

Transferred troops no longer clutter the bottom of the Troops tab.

---

### Sabotage now notifies the target

When your training or build queue is sabotaged, you receive a push
notification telling you which nexus was hit and how many minutes of
delay were added. Embed (spy placement) missions remain silent to the
target , only sabotage triggers an alert.

---

### Travel time shown before sending

The attack form on the Starfield map now displays an estimated travel
time before you confirm. Updates live as you add or remove troops ,
slower units increase the estimate, and overseers apply their 3x
penalty visibly so there are no surprises after you hit send.

---

### Combat reports appear on strike finish

Previously, attackers only saw the combat report after their surviving
troops walked all the way home. Now both attacker and defender see the
report the moment the battle resolves. You no longer have to wait an
hour for your troops to return before finding out what happened.

---

### Faction cap: 10 members

Factions are now hard-capped at 10 players. Invites, join requests, and
approvals all enforce the limit. Any existing faction that was over 10
has been trimmed automatically , lowest-influence members first, leaders
are never removed.

---

### Last Played in faction members tab

Leaders and officers can now see when each member was last active. Shows
as a relative timestamp ("3h ago", "2d ago") next to each name in the
Members tab.

---

### Player profile lists all nexuses

Your profile page now shows every nexus you own , name, specialization,
and influence. Each entry has a coordinate badge that opens the Starfield
map centered on that nexus.

---

### Login button fix on iPhone PWA

The Login and Enlist buttons on the landing page were partially hidden
behind the iOS status bar (clock, wifi, battery) when running as a
home-screen app. Both the landing navbar and the in-game header now
respect the safe-area inset so nothing overlaps system UI.
