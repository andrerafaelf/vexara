## Vexara - Alpha Patch Notes v0.21

You can now tell at a glance who's actually at the controls: nexuses light up
when their commander has just done something. Decommissioning troops gets a
proper, focused dialog instead of a cramped inline panel, Influence is dialed
back a touch, and the crash screen finally looks like it belongs to Vexara.

---

### New: see who's active on the map

Ever scouted a target and wondered whether the owner was asleep or about to
catch you in the act? Now there's a tell.

Whenever a commander **trains, builds, cancels, or decommissions** at a nexus,
that nexus is flagged **active**. Other players see a small **green pulse** on
the nexus marker on the map, and an **Active** badge on the nexus's detail panel
when they inspect it.

A few details, kept honest:

- **It's a 15-minute window.** The pulse lights up on an action and fades on its
  own after 15 minutes of quiet — the same "recently active" idea you'll know
  from other strategy games.
- **It's a presence hint, not a spy report.** It only tells you *someone acted
  recently* — never what they did, what they have, or where they're sending it.
- **Your own current nexus doesn't show it to you.** You already know you're
  here. The signal is for *other* players reading the map.
- **Neutrals never pulse.** Only player-owned nexuses can be active.

Use it to read the room: an active cluster of enemy nexuses is a galaxy that's
awake; a long-dark one is a very different kind of target.

### Decommissioning troops, reworked

Scrapping units used to expand a tight panel **inside** the troop card, which
spilled out of its frame and felt cramped — especially on mobile.

Decommissioning now opens a **dedicated dialog**. It shows the unit you're
scrapping, how many are stationed, a clear amount field with a **Max** button,
and an upfront summary of exactly what you'll pay and gain:

- **Population freed** from disbanding the units, and
- the **10% scrap fee** in biomass / alloy / ore — which, as always, is a fee
  for the room it frees, **not** a refund.

If you can't cover the fee, the dialog says so before you commit. Press **Enter**
to confirm, or tap **Cancel** to back out. Same rules as before — just a far
cleaner, harder-to-misread flow.

### Influence dialed back slightly

**Influence** was climbing a little faster than intended as armies grew. We've
trimmed the headline number by roughly **15%** across the board.

This is an even, across-the-board adjustment: **every** nexus is scaled by the
same amount, so where you sit on the leaderboard relative to everyone else is
unchanged — only the raw number is a touch more grounded. Your standing on the
ladder is exactly where it was.

### A crash screen that looks like Vexara

The rare "something went wrong" error page was using leftover placeholder
styling — wrong colours, rounded corners, nothing like the rest of the game. If
you ever hit it, it now matches the app: gunmetal panel, amber accent, the
signature cut-corner buttons, and a **Try again** / **Go home** pair. When the
error carries a reference code, it's shown so it's easier to report.
