# Neon Dominion - Troops Guide

## Overview

Neon Dominion features 12 distinct troop types, each with specialized roles in combat and resource management. Troops are trained at the **Recruitment Core** (formerly Troop Fabricator) and consume population slots.

---

### Special Units

#### 👑 Overseer

**Role:** Conquest / Control Unit

**Description:** Non-combat command unit required to reduce Control Level and permanently override and capture enemy nexuses. Similar to the Nobleman in Tribal Wars.

**Stats:**

- Attack: 0 (non-combat)
- Defense: 30
- Speed: 15
- Carry Capacity: 0

**Cost:**

- Biomass: 300
- Alloy: 250
- Ore: 200
- Population: 5
- Training Time: 50 minutes

**Requirements:**

- Recruitment Core: Level 5
- Command Core: Level 3
- Shield Grid: Level 2
- **Maximum 1 Overseer per nexus**

**Important Notes:**

- Overseers do NOT participate in combat
- They survive attacks as long as at least one other troop survives
- **If all other troops die, the Overseer dies too** - always send strong escorts!
- Each successful attack with an Overseer reduces the target nexus's Control Level
- When Control Level reaches 0, the nexus is conquered and ownership transfers
- Losing an Overseer is extremely costly - protect them with powerful armies!

**Conquest Mechanics:**

1. Train 1 Overseer at your nexus (takes 50 minutes)
2. Send Overseer with an attacking army
3. Win the battle to reduce enemy Control Level by 1
4. Repeat until Control Level reaches 0
5. Nexus is conquered and becomes yours

## Combat Mechanics

### Attack Power Calculation

- Each troop contributes its **Attack stat × Quantity** to total attack power
- Higher attack power increases chances of victory

### Defense Power Calculation

- Each defending troop contributes its **Defense stat × Quantity** to total defense power
- Shield Grid provides a +2% bonus per level to total defense
- Higher defense power protects your nexus

### Speed Mechanics

- Speed determines travel time between nexuses
- Average speed of attacking army = weighted average of all troops sent
- Faster armies arrive sooner but may be weaker
- Slower armies (with Siege Mechs/Annihilators) are powerful but take longer

### Casualty Calculation

- Losses depend on the power ratio between attacker and defender
- Randomness factor: ±25% variation ensures battles aren't identical
- **Defense Factor:** Troops with lower defense die more easily
  - Breaker Units (15 defense) suffer heavy casualties
  - Siege Mechs (200 defense) have excellent survival rates
- Winner suffers fewer casualties than loser

### Resource Looting

- Only victorious attackers can loot resources
- **Carry Capacity** determines maximum loot:
  - Each troop has individual carry capacity (see stats above)
  - Total capacity = sum of all surviving troops' carry values
- Maximum loot: 50% of defender's resources
- Dead troops can't carry resources back

### Example Battle Calculation

**Attacker:** 100 Assault Troopers (30 attack each) = 3,000 attack power
**Defender:** 50 Siege Mechs (200 defense each) = 10,000 defense power + 10% Shield Grid bonus = 11,000 defense power

**Result:** Defender wins decisively

**Attacker Losses:** ~80% casualties (80 Assault Troopers die)
**Defender Losses:** ~15% casualties (7-8 Siege Mechs die)

## Strategic Tips

### Early Game (Command Core 1-3)

- Focus on Assault Troopers and Lancer Drones
- Train a few Recon Operatives for scouting
- Don't neglect Breaker Units for offensive raids

### Mid Game (Command Core 4-6)

- Unlock Disruptor Units (Lv4 Recruitment Core)
- Train Suppressor Teams (Lv3 Command Core)
- Build up Skimmer Bike fleet for fast raids
- Train Bulwark Guards (Lv5 Life Support) to defend key nexuses

### Late Game (Command Core 7+)

- Train Interceptor Bikes (requires 50 Skimmer Bikes)
- Unlock Annihilator Platforms (Lv6 Command Core)
- Train 1 Overseer per nexus for conquest capability
- Maintain balanced armies with all unit types

### Army Composition Suggestions

**Raiding Party (Fast & Light):**

- 50 Skimmer Bikes
- 30 Interceptor Bikes
- 20 Recon Operatives
- Purpose: Quick strikes, steal resources, escape before defenders arrive

**Balanced Attack Force:**

- 100 Assault Troopers
- 50 Breaker Units
- 30 Suppressor Teams
- 20 Disruptor Units
- 10 Siege Mechs
- Purpose: Solid offensive power with decent speed

**Heavy Siege Army:**

- 50 Siege Mechs
- 20 Annihilator Platforms
- 50 Assault Troopers (escort)
- 1 Overseer (for conquest)
- Purpose: Maximum destruction, nexus conquest, slow but devastating

**Defensive Garrison:**

- 100 Bulwark Guards
- 50 Assault Troopers
- 30 Lancer Drones
- 20 Siege Mechs
- Purpose: Hold the line, maximize defensive power

**Scout Mission:**

- 1 Recon Operative
- Purpose: Gather intelligence on enemy nexus before attacking

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why can I only train 1 Overseer per nexus?**
A: Overseers are extremely powerful conquest units. Limiting them to 1 per nexus ensures conquest requires strategic planning and multiple coordinated attacks.

**Q: What happens if my Overseer dies in battle?**
A: Overseers have 0 attack and don't participate in combat directly. They survive as long as at least one other troop survives. **However, if your entire army is wiped out, the Overseer dies with them.** Always send sufficient escorts to protect your Overseer! If the attack fails but troops survive, the Overseer returns home safely, but no Control Level reduction occurs.

**Q: How many attacks with an Overseer does it take to conquer a nexus?**
A: Each nexus starts with a Control Level (typically 100). Each successful attack with an Overseer reduces this by 1. So approximately 100 successful attacks are needed, though this may vary based on game balance.

**Q: Can I scout without Recon Operatives?**
A: No, Recon Operative is the only scout unit. Always train some before attacking unknown targets!

**Q: What's the best troop for farming resources?**
A: Skimmer Bikes (60 carry) and Annihilator Platforms (100 carry) have the highest carry capacities. For speed, use Skimmer Bikes.

**Q: Do Suppressor Teams weaken enemies before battle?**
A: No, the "weakening" is abstracted into their attack value (60). Think of them as applying debuffs during the battle calculation.

**Q: Can Disruptor Units one-shot Siege Mechs?**
A: No, but they're effective against them due to their anti-armor design. They contribute extra damage against mechanical units in combat calculations.

## Building Your Empire

Remember: Troops are your primary means of expansion and defense. Balance your economy (resources and production) with military power. A strong economy supports a strong army, but a strong army protects your economy.

**Population Management:**

- Each troop takes 1-5 population slots
- Upgrade Life Support and Command Core to increase max population
- Balance civilian population (workers) with military population

**Resource Investment:**

- Basic troops are cheap and quick to train
- Elite troops require significant resource investment
- Plan your troop composition based on your strategic goals

**Time Management:**

- Basic troops: 10-15 minutes
- Advanced troops: 20-25 minutes
- Elite troops: 25-40 minutes
- Overseer: 50 minutes

Train during downtime and overnight to maintain steady military growth!
